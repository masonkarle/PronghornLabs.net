# Fireweed

**Every link opens in the right place.**

Fireweed is a web browser built on Safari’s engine. Spaces such as Personal and Work each keep their own logins, favorites and pinned tabs, so signing in at work never signs you in at home. Rules send a site to the Space, or the other browser, it belongs in, every time.

### Spaces and rules
- Each Space has its own cookies, logins, favorites and pinned tabs.
- Rules open a site in a Space or another browser, and each move shows a note with Undo.
- Burn closes a tab and clears its site, or clears a whole Space. Fireproof sites stay signed in.
- Private tabs keep nothing once they close.

### Reader
- Save any page to read later, in clean type, on all your devices.
- Keep articles offline, organise them in folders and tags, and search their text.
- Listen reads articles aloud in a natural voice that runs on your device.
- Import from Raindrop.io, Pocket and Instapaper.

No account, no ads and no tracking.
