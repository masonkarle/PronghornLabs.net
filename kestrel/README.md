# Kestrel

**A stage timer for speakers.**

Kestrel shows you, in large numbers you can read from the lectern, how long is left of the part you’re on. Split a talk into segments, run a whole programme item by item, or set a countdown on the spot. Colour only ever means something: amber to wrap up, red when you’re over, and a word beside it as well.

### Three kinds of timer
- **Segmented.** A total time split into segments, each a fixed length or a weighted share of what is left. Finish a segment early with Next and the time saved is spread over the segments still to come. End by a time of day: start late and the weighted segments shrink to fit.
- **Program.** An agenda in sections, run by a chair. Kestrel shows how far ahead or behind you are, the planned and projected finish, and what each item actually took.
- **Quick.** A countdown set on the spot, or a stopwatch.

### On stage
- Countdown, stopwatch or clock, switchable while the timer runs. Over time counts on in red.
- On Mac, an always-on-top floating timer, and keyboard shortcuts for Start, Next and Reset.
- Your timers, and a run in progress, stay in step across your devices through your own iCloud.
- Meeting templates for the Watchtower, CBS, Public Talk and the midweek meeting, if you turn them on.

Kestrel has no accounts, no ads and collects nothing.
